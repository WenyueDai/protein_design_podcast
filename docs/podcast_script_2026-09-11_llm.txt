The first shift comes from a maize meiosis protocol that moves crossover detection from low‑resolution genetic maps or cytological spreads to genome‑wide, base‑pair‑level profiling using chromatin immunoprecipitation‑sequencing. The fourth shift involves AI‑guided discovery of dual P‑gp/BCRP inhibitors from marine natural product fragments.
