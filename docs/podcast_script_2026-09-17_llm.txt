Finally, DeepEffector frames effector prediction as a language‑model problem for fungi and oomycetes, but the snippet didn’t say which architectural choices — transformer depth, tokenization scheme, or training objective — gave the best performance on the held‑out test set. Running an ablation where you swap the masked language model for a contrastive loss, or where you reduce the model from 12 to six layers, and then measuring the change in area‑under‑the‑ROC curve for effector versus non‑effector classification would pinpoint which design choices are truly driving the reported gains. Each of these threads opens a concrete experiment, a measurable metric, and a clear path forward without re‑hashing what we’ve already heard.
