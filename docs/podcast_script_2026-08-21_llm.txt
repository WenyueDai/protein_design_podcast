All of these threads feel like they could turn into short, focused projects — maybe a weekend of scripting, a small benchmark run, or a literature dive into the cited AlphaFold2, OAS, AlphaBind, and ESM papers — and each would help us sharpen the intuition for where these new tools shine and where they still need a human in the loop.
