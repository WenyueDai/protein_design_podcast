Computational experiments to run later: saturating mutagenesis of recombinase DNA‑binding domains coupled to machine‑learning prediction of off‑target sites; graph‑neural‑network refinement of TDA features on noisy surface meshes; quantum‑mechanical scoring of Zn‑peptide coordination geometries; network‑propagation models linking chaperone expression to clinical endpoints; and longitudinal mixed‑effects modeling of dSST trajectories under GLP‑1RA therapy.  

These open questions keep the conversation moving beyond what the papers explicitly state.
